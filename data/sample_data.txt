This folder holds runtime data generated by the application:

  shop_config.txt   - the shop's licence number, owner name and licensed
                      limit (kg). Created automatically on first run with
                      placeholder values; edit it to match your actual
                      PESO licence, or delete it to be re-prompted.

  stock.dat         - the current inventory, one line per batch:
                      id,category,batchNo,quantityKg,expiryDate

  transactions.log  - an append-only history of every ADD, SALE,
                      RESTOCK and REMOVE action, with a timestamp.

Sample shop_config.txt and stock.dat content, for reference
(category codes: 0=Sparklers 1=Flowerpots 2=Chakris 3=Rockets 4=Bombs 5=Other):

shop_config.txt:
  PESO-MH-2026-0147,Deepa Traders,250.00

stock.dat:
  1,0,SPK-001,40.00,20-12-2026
  2,3,RKT-014,55.50,18-12-2026
  3,4,BMB-007,12.00,15-12-2026

These three lines would put total stock at 107.5 kg against a 250 kg
licence (SAFE status). Copy the two blocks above into shop_config.txt
and stock.dat respectively if you want to start the application with
sample data already loaded, rather than from an empty inventory.
